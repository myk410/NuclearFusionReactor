# Portfolio distinctness audit

32 concepts: 18 plant candidates, 12 enabling interventions and 2 scientific testbeds. This is **not** 32 independent complete reactor designs. No novelty is claimed. The portfolio includes mechanisms with weak evidence to permit explicit rejection rather than silently omit them.

Tokamak and spherical tokamak differ principally in the central-column/beta constraint, and are related. Direct drive, heavy-ion drive and fast ignition differ in driver/coupling physics, but share inertial target and repetition risks. Beam-sustained and compression-driven FRCs differ in sustainment and work-recovery balance, not just shape. They must not count as statistically independent success routes.

- C001 (plant): High-field D–T tokamak — falsifier: Reject a proposed compact envelope if shielding or current-drive input removes its electric margin.
- C002 (plant): Optimized D–T stellarator — falsifier: A current-drive saving fails if added transport/heating and maintenance costs are larger.
- C003 (plant): Spherical tokamak with protected central column — falsifier: Discard a geometry if no central-column lifetime and breeding margin survive the space allocation.
- C004 (plant): Magnetized liner inertial fusion — falsifier: Reject gain extrapolation that omits measured mix or uses peak yield without consumable energy.
- C005 (plant): Liquid-liner magnetized target fusion — falsifier: Fail if target cooling or compression asymmetry prevents sufficient yield after full mechanical-drive accounting.
- C006 (plant): Direct-drive laser inertial fusion — falsifier: Fail if practical illumination tolerance and efficiency jointly imply Q below electrical threshold.
- C007 (plant): Heavy-ion driven inertial fusion — falsifier: Fail if transported beam quality requires more wall energy than the gain advantage can repay.
- C008 (plant): Fast-ignition inertial fusion — falsifier: Fail if measured useful ignition coupling stays too small to close the combined wall balance.
- C009 (plant): Compression-driven field-reversed configuration — falsifier: Fail if transient improvements do not persist through a full formation-compression-reset cycle.
- C010 (plant): Beam-sustained field-reversed configuration — falsifier: Fail if the beam support power required at reactor scale grows faster than useful fusion output.
- C011 (plant): HTS magnetic mirror with end-loss recovery — falsifier: Fail if optimistic end-loss recovery is required merely to offset a non-burning plasma.
- C012 (plant): Sheared-flow-stabilized Z pinch — falsifier: Fail if neutron interpretation or stability duration cannot support reactor-scale gain extrapolation.
- C013 (plant): Dense plasma focus with kinetic population accounting — falsifier: Fail if apparent temperature is a small fast-ion tail with insufficient integrated fusion energy.
- C014 (plant): Inertial electrostatic confinement with space-charge control — falsifier: Fail if the confining-electron budget or ion collision losses exceed fusion power even ideally.
- C015 (plant): Muon-catalyzed D–T fusion with complete source accounting — falsifier: Fail if measured useful cycles cannot repay measured source electricity, independent of fusion at low bulk temperature.
- C016 (plant): Nonthermal proton–boron fusion with alpha channeling — falsifier: Fail if a self-consistent kinetic balance requires more distribution-maintenance electricity than useful fusion output.
- C017 (plant): Photon-driven lattice fusion with cascade accounting — falsifier: Fail if measured complete source-to-product nuclear energy is below even an ideal conversion threshold.
- C018 (plant): Electrochemically loaded beam–target fusion with energy recycling — falsifier: Fail if the maximum recoverable loss reduction remains orders short of measured whole-system deficit.
- C019 (enabler): Advanced divertor with impurity-aware exhaust control — falsifier: Fail if apparent heat relief simply exports loss into the core or removes electrical margin.
- C020 (enabler): Renewable liquid plasma-facing surface — falsifier: Fail if evaporated impurities or pumping costs exceed the wall-lifetime gain.
- C021 (enabler): Low-inventory tritium processing and direct recycle — falsifier: Fail if energy saved by recycle is outweighed by impurity-driven plasma loss or unaccounted tritium.
- C022 (enabler): Blanket flow-path and electrical-insulation optimization — falsifier: Fail if electrical insulation lowers pumping but makes wall temperature or reliability unacceptable.
- C023 (enabler): High-temperature power cycle matched to heat grades — falsifier: Fail if high nominal cycle efficiency requires unqualified materials or unavailable source temperatures.
- C024 (enabler): Irradiation-tolerant structural material qualification — falsifier: Fail if improved swelling resistance destroys ductility, thermal conductivity or manufacturability.
- C025 (enabler): Remote-maintenance architecture for cycle-average export — falsifier: Fail if modular access sacrifices so much blanket or shielding coverage that availability savings vanish.
- C026 (enabler): Pulse-energy recovery and storage-loss minimization — falsifier: Fail if advertised recovery counts the same inductive energy before and after a pulse or omits storage losses.
- C027 (enabler): Magnet thermal-shield and neutron-shield co-optimization — falsifier: Fail if reduced refrigerator load requires enough extra plant size or lost breeding volume to erase the benefit.
- C028 (enabler): Helium ash removal with fuel-selective exhaust — falsifier: Fail if ash extraction also removes enough hot fuel to increase recirculating power above the saved dilution penalty.
- C029 (enabler): Alpha-channeling power redistribution in D–T plasmas — falsifier: Fail if required wave electricity or alpha escape exceeds heating and ash-removal benefits.
- C030 (testbed): Density–screening identifiability in loaded solids — falsifier: Reject the screening hypothesis if density and stopping alone predict held-out enhancement within uncertainty.
- C031 (testbed): Nuclear-product and calorimetry consistency analysis — falsifier: Reject a specific nuclear interpretation when its quantitatively predicted products are absent at adequate sensitivity.
- C032 (enabler): Whole-plant exergy and reliability co-optimization — falsifier: Fail if proposed savings disappear when heat grades, downtime and shared component constraints are enforced together.
