# Interim feasibility assessment — C002: Optimized D–T stellarator

**Status: preliminary, not the project's final comprehensive report.** Date: 2026-11-24. Current plant rank: 2; comparative score: 72.5/100. Evidence confidence: moderate. Scores are judgments, not success probabilities. Full dossier: [C document](../TheoreticalConcepts/C002.md); focused audit: [leading-ten review](../Research/LeadingTen/C002.md).

## Scientific case
The stellarator deserves nearly equal priority: eliminating bulk toroidal-current sustainment may reduce circulating power and current-driven disruption exposure. The main uncertainty moves into optimized 3D confinement and geometry-dependent integration.

The Infinity Two blanket study supplies an actual integration calculation. Its low-fidelity breeder comparison is stronger than a claim that blanket space will simply be found, but remains conditional on geometry and operating losses.

## Quantitative basis available now
The executed E001 balance is x=eta_t(c M+r/Q)-1/(eta_d Q)-a. Under the explicitly illustrative eta_t=.4, c=.95, M=1, r=0 and a=.08, Q_min=6.6667 at eta_d=.5, 22.2222 at .15 and 333.3333 at .01. These **are not parameters assigned to this concept**. They show why its actual driver, capture and auxiliary data must be obtained before calculating a plant-specific margin.

The statistical parameter boxes and ranking perturbations in [computational findings](../Simulations/Findings.md) are executed, reproducible scenario exercises. They cannot supply attainable Q, actual conversion efficiency, maintenance life or economic viability. No reactor-specific plasma simulation or neutron-transport calculation was run here.

## Strongest counterargument
Absence of driven toroidal current can reduce a load without establishing adequate alpha confinement, exhaust or shielding. A non-burning long pulse does not prove burning steady state.

Averages may hide localized magnet exposure; a nominal breeding surplus can be consumed by penetrations, processing delays and reserve inventory.

## What would change the decision
Demote if alpha transport, local magnet damage or blanket access eliminates the saved-power advantage across credible uncertainties. Promote only when new evidence closes those specific gaps.

## Highest-value next investigation
Use the blanket-study model as a prior-art framework, not as a validated result; insert real penetrations and local radiation constraints, then compare current-drive saving against added coil, access and maintenance costs.

Calculate the net-electric boundary with current-drive savings separated from additional 3D coil, refrigeration and maintenance burdens; include port-resolved neutron transport before treating modeled breeding margin as assured.

## Integration and qualification gates
Before advancing to an experimentally defensible plant claim, establish a measured or independently validated confinement/loss basis, compatible exhaust and materials limits, a full electricity/fuel-processing ledger, and positive energy over maintenance cycles. For a D–T host, fuel self-sufficiency must include reserve inventory, decay, processing losses and deployment growth rather than a nominal TBR target alone. Report the physical uncertainty and the decision sensitivity separately.

Cost and materials-resource models have not been produced for this concept. No LCOE, deployment date, experimental endorsement or probability is provided. Shared sources and shared engineering dependencies mean alternatives are not independent draws from a success distribution.
