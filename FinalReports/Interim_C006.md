## Scientific case
Direct drive offers the possibility of avoiding some indirect coupling losses, with demanding illumination and instability requirements. The reviewed hydro-equivalent evidence must remain separate from an experimentally burning, repeated direct-drive system.

The direct-drive study uses hydro-equivalent scaling. Its title must not be shortened into a claim of achieved burning at the actual experimental scale. A publisher correction is identified and remains an explicit retrieval task.

## Strongest counterargument
Transferring scaled hotspot conditions to ignition and plant output entails intensity-dependent laser-plasma coupling, asymmetry and driver-efficiency assumptions.

Small target imperfections, nonuniformity and repetitive optics replacement can invalidate favorable single-shot scaling.

## What would change the decision
Demote if realistic coupling, imperfections and optical lifetime remove its proposed efficiency advantage. Promote only when scaling assumptions and driver/target compatibility receive new experimental support.

## Highest-value next investigation

Test how coupled changes in target gain and wall efficiency shift eta_d Q; do not improve eta_d independently of the temporal/spectral driver requirements.
