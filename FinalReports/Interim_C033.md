# Interim feasibility assessment — C033: Indirect-drive inertial fusion with an efficient repetitive driver

**Status: preliminary, not the project's final comprehensive report.** Date: 2026-11-24. Current plant rank: 3; comparative score: 66.0/100. Evidence confidence: low. Scores are judgments, not success probabilities. Full dossier: [C document](../TheoreticalConcepts/C033.md); focused audit: [leading-ten review](../Research/LeadingTen/C033.md).

## Scientific case
Indirect drive has unusually strong target-level evidence, but a power station needs a different demonstration: sustained repetition with driver and target production energy included. The target milestone supports serious investigation without proving the plant.

Indirect-drive experiments furnish the clearest achieved target-gain milestone in this source set. That specific achievement is why omission of this baseline from the first portfolio needed correction.

## Quantitative basis available now
The executed E001 balance is x=eta_t(c M+r/Q)-1/(eta_d Q)-a. Under the explicitly illustrative eta_t=.4, c=.95, M=1, r=0 and a=.08, Q_min=6.6667 at eta_d=.5, 22.2222 at .15 and 333.3333 at .01. These **are not parameters assigned to this concept**. They show why its actual driver, capture and auxiliary data must be obtained before calculating a plant-specific margin.

The statistical parameter boxes and ranking perturbations in [computational findings](../Simulations/Findings.md) are executed, reproducible scenario exercises. They cannot supply attainable Q, actual conversion efficiency, maintenance life or economic viability. No reactor-specific plasma simulation or neutron-transport calculation was run here.

## Strongest counterargument
A hohlraum adds a conversion/coupling chain. Replacing a low-efficiency research laser with a hypothetical efficient driver cannot be assumed to preserve the target irradiation history and gain.

Target supply, chamber clearing, driver optical damage, low repetition and maintenance remain in the electric ledger even if every individual shot has target gain above unity.

## What would change the decision
Demote if practical driver efficiency and reproducible target gain have no overlapping region above electrical breakeven. Do not confuse improving single-shot yield with closing that overlap.

## Highest-value next investigation
Test whether a compatible efficient driver and repeatable target together can exceed the E001 threshold with chamber/optical maintenance included. A separate efficient-laser result plus a separate high-gain shot cannot be multiplied without evidence of compatibility.

Use separate efficiencies for wall-to-laser, laser-to-radiation and radiation-to-capsule, with a single consistent target-gain boundary; derive feasible combined ranges before assigning useful output.

## Integration and qualification gates
Before advancing to an experimentally defensible plant claim, establish a measured or independently validated confinement/loss basis, compatible exhaust and materials limits, a full electricity/fuel-processing ledger, and positive energy over maintenance cycles. For a D–T host, fuel self-sufficiency must include reserve inventory, decay, processing losses and deployment growth rather than a nominal TBR target alone. Report the physical uncertainty and the decision sensitivity separately.

Cost and materials-resource models have not been produced for this concept. No LCOE, deployment date, experimental endorsement or probability is provided. Shared sources and shared engineering dependencies mean alternatives are not independent draws from a success distribution.
