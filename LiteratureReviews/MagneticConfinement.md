# Magnetic confinement — initial critical synthesis

**Established principles.** Magnetic fields constrain charged-particle motion but do not eliminate collisions, heat transport, radiation or neutron escape. Plasma gain must be converted into a plant-electric ledger. High field increases magnetic pressure and may improve compactness at fixed beta; structural and neutron-shielding constraints oppose unlimited miniaturization.

**Accomplishments.** R018 establishes a substantial D–T energy pulse at JET. R007 supplies long-duration stellarator confinement evidence. R021 supplies high-field magnet component evidence. These are different observables and cannot be merged into a fictitious machine that simultaneously demonstrates them all.

**Current limitations.** Tokamak sustainment and disruptions, stellarator energetic-particle confinement and three-dimensional access, and spherical central-column constraints dominate different portions of the engineering problem. The R023 current ARC study is a design prediction, with risks intended to be tested through other facilities. R024 is also a model, not fuel-cycle operation.

**Open questions.** What jointly achievable confinement, radiated-power fraction, divertor state, ash fraction and magnet lifetime remain after full integration? What happens under adverse covariance rather than independently favorable parameters?

**Competing explanations.** A measured performance improvement may reflect heating deposition, impurity reduction or diagnostic calibration rather than transport improvement. Confinement extrapolation cannot be justified by matching one scalar parameter alone.

**Historical failures.** Disruptions and high transport motivated continuing optimization rather than demonstrating that all toroidal devices fail. This session did not perform an adequate primary-source search of historical failures; no exhaustive historical conclusion is drawn.

**Recent developments.** R023 updates an older high-field point design; R024 studies blanket integration. Their quantitative claims remain conditional on engineering fidelity.

**Cross-disciplinary connection.** Coil geometry, blanket chemistry, cryogenic exergy and maintenance access form one constrained problem. Evaluate C001–C003 with C021–C027 and C032 as dependencies, not additive score bonuses.
