# Comparative scoring methodology — S001

Weights: physics 20%, net-electric potential 20%, experiment 15%, theory 10%, engineering 10%, unresolved barriers 10%, scale 5%, materials/resources 5%, economics 5%. Each score is 0–10; total is sum(weight percent × score/10). A demonstrated physical-law violation imposes rejection independent of total. Lack of evidence alone is not a law violation.

Anchors: 0 = absent or incompatible; 2 = major unsupported extrapolation; 5 = plausible component/mechanism with major integration gaps; 8 = strong support with important residual qualification; 10 = compelling compatibility or well-established performance for the criterion. For net-electric potential, even the leaders are projections; 10 would require integrated evidence not available here. For barriers, higher is better. Evidence and theory are separate; an elegant model earns no experimental points.

Every criterion has a rationale in each C### dossier and in Research/ConceptData.json, the machine-readable scoring source of truth. All scores are preliminary subjective judgments by one AI analysis, not independent expert consensus. Evidence-access limitations lower confidence. Economics scores lack costing and cannot support investment conclusions. Evidence maturity is not assigned a fabricated TRL.

Master.csv contains an overall arithmetic ordering of all types, while Master.md separates plant candidates from conditional interventions and testbeds. The plant top-ten and top-five selections use plant candidates only; otherwise a mature turbine could misleadingly become the preferred fusion source. Research priority is a separate subjective value-of-information judgment and does not equal feasibility.

Sensitivity: ComputationalModels/analyze.py varies weights 1000 times and evaluates three named alternative profiles; see Mathematics/ModelSpecification.md. This does not address uncertain scores, correlated criteria or incomplete literature. Differences under about five points should not drive decisions without deeper evidence. Next: elicit criterion intervals, test dominance, and assess architecture-specific resource and cost models.

Impact-if-validated tiers remain qualitative and do not satisfy a complete quantitative impact ranking. Changes belong in Rankings/Changes.md with previous/new scores and the triggering evidence. Never revise scores merely to preserve an interesting conclusion.
