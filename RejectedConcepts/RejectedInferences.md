# Rejected hypotheses and inferences — preserve exact scope

**RJ001 / rejected:** target gain Q>1 alone proves a net-electric facility. It omits driver wall efficiency, conversion and auxiliaries; E001 supplies a direct counterexample. This does not reject inertial fusion.

**RJ002 / rejected:** a 15% beam–target rate increase implies useful energy gain. Multiplying an unknown deficient absolute gain by 1.15 gives no breakeven conclusion. This does not reject the measured enhancement or all electrochemical research.

**RJ003 / rejected:** a cool solid target proves the reacting nuclei had only room-temperature thermal kinetic energies. Externally accelerated or photon-produced energetic species invalidate that inference. This does not reject quantum tunneling or screening.

**RJ004 / rejected:** recovered plasma input is fusion-born electrical export. R014 explicitly distinguishes these. Electricity in a lamp is insufficient to identify its energy source or net plant balance.

**RJ005 / rejected:** a solver termination flag proves a physical operating point. R028 illustrates a residual/power-balance failure; numerical convergence alone is not an existence proof.

**RJ006 / rejected:** heat recirculation, screening or catalysis can eliminate the need for energy conservation. No favorable aggregate score can rescue a mechanism that creates energy without a specified source. No particular disputed publication is fully adjudicated by this general rejection.

**Not yet rejected:** C014, C016, C017 and C018 remain low-ranked research concepts; a full quantitative no-go result over every physically allowed variant has not been derived. Claims requiring unspecified new phenomena must be labelled and withheld from favorable ranking until validated.
