# Research charter and acceptance criteria

This project investigates sustained usable net electrical fusion energy through a continuing multidisciplinary programme. Its goal includes new falsifiable hypotheses and computational analysis as well as primary-source synthesis. Physics, chemistry, engineering and mathematical reasoning must be connected explicitly when they bear on a proposed mechanism; no single specialty is presumed sufficient.

## Acceptance gates by stage

| Stage | Required evidence for completion | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Foundations | Permanent instructions, source taxonomy, accounting derivation, reproducibility conventions and continuity | Infrastructure established; scientific baseline continues to expand |
| 2 — Literature | Broad primary and contrary-evidence coverage of every requested area, historical failures, recent corrections and source access depth | Initial thematic synthesis only; incomplete |
| 3 — Concepts | At least 30 distinct, identified dossiers with 20 required content areas, mechanism distinctions and falsifiers | 33 first-pass dossiers created; substantive source validation remains |
| 4 — Evaluation | Criteria-specific rationale, uncertainty and attempted disproof for every concept | Initial screen complete in form; evidence quality needs further audit |
| 5 — Computation | Reproducible analysis with units, assumptions, constants, executed results and model limitations; fit/validate against experimental data where appropriate | Accounting, synthetic uncertainty, inference and tradeoff calculations executed; calibrated plant models absent |
| 6 — Comparison | Prescribed weighted scoring, physics gate, multiple ranking views and sensitivity; documented changes | Preliminary rankings and weight/score sensitivity; quantitative impact ranking unresolved |
| 7 — Leading ten | Substantially deeper literature, mathematical tests and unresolved-question investigation for ten leading candidates | Ten focused audit notes created; extensive deep review incomplete |
| 8 — Criticism | Skeptical cross-disciplinary tests, falsification and correction records; genuinely independent scrutiny labelled accurately | Single-analysis criticism only; independent expert review absent |
| 9 — Refinement | Evidence-triggered score changes and additional meaningful hypotheses, retained rejected work | Coverage correction and four hypotheses recorded; continuing |
| 10 — Conclusions | Comprehensive top-five feasibility reports, calibrated comparative reasoning and evidence-qualified final direction | Five interim assessments and interim synthesis only; final reports pending |

## Scope and deliverable controls
The concept count includes complete plant candidates, enabling interventions and scientific testbeds. They are labelled and ranked separately for interpretation; do not claim 33 complete designs. Every dossier includes summary/principles/literature/theory/math/energy/assumptions/evidence/counterevidence/obstacles/questions/advantages/failure/breakthroughs/validation and feasibility/confidence ratings. Similar driver or geometry families share dependencies.

Required research extends to conventional and unconventional confinement, electrochemistry and Thunderbird, muons, solid-state targets, screening/tunneling, fuel cycles, magnets, stability, conversion, heat, irradiation, abandoned experiments and emerging science. Each theme must eventually document principles, accomplishments, limitations, unresolved questions, competing explanations, failures, updates and interdisciplinary connections at primary-source depth.

Scores follow the user's exact nine weights. Separate evidence quality, scientific uncertainty, maturity, research potential, originality and dependence on new phenomena from total score. A favorable arithmetic total cannot rescue a physical-law violation. Maintain individual score rationale, independent ranking views and explicit change history. Never label numerical ratings as empirical probabilities.

No construction or operation instructions, new physical law, reactor validation or inevitable energy breakthrough is an acceptance requirement. Scientific accuracy and a useful falsification take precedence over an exciting conclusion. Preserve unfinished work and permit subsequent sessions to proceed without rebuilding the same foundation.
