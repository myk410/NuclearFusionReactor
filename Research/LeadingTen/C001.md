# Focused leading-candidate audit — C001: High-field D–T tokamak

This is a deeper **first audit**, not a completed extensive feasibility review. Current comparative score: 73.0/100. It is a single-analysis critical review, not independent peer review. See the full [concept dossier](../../TheoreticalConcepts/C001.md).

## Additional source question
References: R023 R006 R021. Exact URLs and access limits are in [Bibliography](../../References/Bibliography.md).

The 2026 ARC V3A study states a new plant design and explicitly identifies unresolved physics risks. Its proposed electric output belongs in the projection column, not experimental evidence. Older ARC/SPARC numbers describe different devices and dates.

## Challenge to the theoretical foundation
The B^4 power-density argument hides fixed-beta and fixed-temperature assumptions. Increased field can change coil stress, shield geometry and achievable operating space; these must be constrained jointly.

## Required mathematical investigation
Write a residual-tested balance that links confinement uncertainty, current drive, exhaust and actual heat grades to the grid meter. Require empirical bounds rather than simultaneously selecting optimistic values.

The executed E001 model supplies only a necessary electrical threshold. It supplies no architecture-specific confinement result. A future coupled model must include input bounds, units, source provenance, numerical residuals, sensitivity and an independent algebraic balance. Matching one published optimum is inadequate calibration.

## Skeptical integration review
Cryogenic load, centre/inboard shielding, blanket coverage, plasma radiation and replacement outages can overwhelm the apparent compactness benefit.

Require a common comparison service: delivered electricity across at least a maintenance cycle. Report net on-state and mean net separately. State whether target supply, cryogenics and fuel processing are on-site or external; externalizing the load cannot improve the expanded system balance.

## Decision and outstanding evidence
Do not increase its evidence score merely because a new design paper predicts net electricity. Its 0.5-point advantage over the stellarator is below reasonable scoring resolution.

Outstanding: full relevant papers and supplements; a contradictory-results search; calibrated parameter distributions; cost/resource inventory; a robust region of coupled energy closure. Until these exist, no plant-specific success probability or comprehensive feasibility conclusion is justified.
