# Focused leading-candidate audit — C002: Optimized D–T stellarator

This is a deeper **first audit**, not a completed extensive feasibility review. Current comparative score: 72.5/100. It is a single-analysis critical review, not independent peer review. See the full [concept dossier](../../TheoreticalConcepts/C002.md).

## Additional source question
References: R024 R007. Exact URLs and access limits are in [Bibliography](../../References/Bibliography.md).

The Infinity Two blanket study supplies an actual integration calculation. Its low-fidelity breeder comparison is stronger than a claim that blanket space will simply be found, but remains conditional on geometry and operating losses.

## Challenge to the theoretical foundation
Absence of driven toroidal current can reduce a load without establishing adequate alpha confinement, exhaust or shielding. A non-burning long pulse does not prove burning steady state.

## Required mathematical investigation
Calculate the net-electric boundary with current-drive savings separated from additional 3D coil, refrigeration and maintenance burdens; include port-resolved neutron transport before treating modeled breeding margin as assured.

The executed E001 model supplies only a necessary electrical threshold. It supplies no architecture-specific confinement result. A future coupled model must include input bounds, units, source provenance, numerical residuals, sensitivity and an independent algebraic balance. Matching one published optimum is inadequate calibration.

## Skeptical integration review
Averages may hide localized magnet exposure; a nominal breeding surplus can be consumed by penetrations, processing delays and reserve inventory.

Require a common comparison service: delivered electricity across at least a maintenance cycle. Report net on-state and mean net separately. State whether target supply, cryogenics and fuel processing are on-site or external; externalizing the load cannot improve the expanded system balance.

## Decision and outstanding evidence
Keep the candidate alongside the tokamak; additional integration evidence raises research priority without resolving reactor-scale performance.

Outstanding: full relevant papers and supplements; a contradictory-results search; calibrated parameter distributions; cost/resource inventory; a robust region of coupled energy closure. Until these exist, no plant-specific success probability or comprehensive feasibility conclusion is justified.
