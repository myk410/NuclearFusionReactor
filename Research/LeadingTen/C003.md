# Focused leading-candidate audit — C003: Spherical tokamak with protected central column

This is a deeper **first audit**, not a completed extensive feasibility review. Current comparative score: 65.0/100. It is a single-analysis critical review, not independent peer review. See the full [concept dossier](../../TheoreticalConcepts/C003.md).

## Additional source question
References: R029 R017. Exact URLs and access limits are in [Bibliography](../../References/Bibliography.md).

The systems study discusses uncertainty when extrapolating confinement to highly radiative power-plant plasmas. Super-X tests are relevant subsystem evidence, but do not validate a protected central column or full breeding configuration.

## Challenge to the theoretical foundation
High beta and reduced size cannot be optimized separately from structural space, current drive and radiation-compatible confinement. A free optimizer can choose a favorable design by moving other uncertain variables.

## Required mathematical investigation
Sweep a constrained radial-build budget and current-drive burden, then propagate lifetime-driven outages. Evaluate whether a broad feasible region survives rather than reporting a single optimum.

The executed E001 model supplies only a necessary electrical threshold. It supplies no architecture-specific confinement result. A future coupled model must include input bounds, units, source provenance, numerical residuals, sensitivity and an independent algebraic balance. Matching one published optimum is inadequate calibration.

## Skeptical integration review
Column replacement could dominate availability; extra shield or exhaust volume may remove the compactness advantage.

Require a common comparison service: delivered electricity across at least a maintenance cycle. Report net on-state and mean net separately. State whether target supply, cryogenics and fuel processing are on-site or external; externalizing the load cannot improve the expanded system balance.

## Decision and outstanding evidence
No quantitative score revision from one abstract-level systems paper. Confidence stays low pending full model and primary-source audit.

Outstanding: full relevant papers and supplements; a contradictory-results search; calibrated parameter distributions; cost/resource inventory; a robust region of coupled energy closure. Until these exist, no plant-specific success probability or comprehensive feasibility conclusion is justified.
