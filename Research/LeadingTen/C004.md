# Focused leading-candidate audit — C004: Magnetized liner inertial fusion

This is a deeper **first audit**, not a completed extensive feasibility review. Current comparative score: 59.0/100. It is a single-analysis critical review, not independent peer review. See the full [concept dossier](../../TheoreticalConcepts/C004.md).

## Additional source question
References: R025 R012. Exact URLs and access limits are in [Bibliography](../../References/Bibliography.md).

Mix is backed by an experiment-specific adverse mechanism rather than a generic instability warning. The Sandia-hosted abstract is useful but its bibliographic metadata should be checked at the original publisher.

## Challenge to the theoretical foundation
Magnetic insulation does not guarantee more fusion if material mix raises radiation or end losses remove energy. Clean two-dimensional predictions must be treated separately from measured mixed targets.

## Required mathematical investigation
Represent mix-induced loss as a constrained penalty, then ask how much higher yield is required to pay for liner and pulsed-driver replacement. Use total recharge energy, not merely energy coupled into fuel.

The executed E001 model supplies only a necessary electrical threshold. It supplies no architecture-specific confinement result. A future coupled model must include input bounds, units, source provenance, numerical residuals, sensitivity and an independent algebraic balance. Matching one published optimum is inadequate calibration.

## Skeptical integration review
Repetition, electrode/transmission-line life and debris reset remain even after a successful burn.

Require a common comparison service: delivered electricity across at least a maintenance cycle. Report net on-state and mean net separately. State whether target supply, cryogenics and fuel processing are on-site or external; externalizing the load cannot improve the expanded system balance.

## Decision and outstanding evidence
Do not promote MagLIF on a material-substitution yield ratio alone; a relative improvement is not absolute electrical closure.

Outstanding: full relevant papers and supplements; a contradictory-results search; calibrated parameter distributions; cost/resource inventory; a robust region of coupled energy closure. Until these exist, no plant-specific success probability or comprehensive feasibility conclusion is justified.
