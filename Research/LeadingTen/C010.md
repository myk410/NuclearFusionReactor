This is a deeper **first audit**, not a completed extensive feasibility review. Current comparative score: 53.0/100. It is a single-analysis critical review, not independent peer review. See the full [concept dossier](../../TheoreticalConcepts/C010.md).

## Additional source question
References: R019 R011. Exact URLs and access limits are in [Bibliography](../../References/Bibliography.md).

A wave can measurably modify ion energies without proving a favorable distribution-maintenance balance. The Rider source is currently a retrieval lead, so no universal prohibition is inferred from it.

## Challenge to the theoretical foundation
A beam-maintained distribution can be mathematically consistent while requiring more electricity than fusion conversion returns. Beam power, particle loss and self-generated fields are coupled.

## Required mathematical investigation
Compare fusion increase against additional wall-beam power at fixed non-beam loads; derive the required marginal fusion/beam-power ratio before detailed kinetic modelling.

The executed E001 model supplies only a necessary electrical threshold. It supplies no architecture-specific confinement result. A future coupled model must include input bounds, units, source provenance, numerical residuals, sensitivity and an independent algebraic balance. Matching one published optimum is inadequate calibration.

## Skeptical integration review
Wall-plug beam efficiency, impurity sources and thermalization can remove the benefit inferred from a high-energy tail.

Require a common comparison service: delivered electricity across at least a maintenance cycle. Report net on-state and mean net separately. State whether target supply, cryogenics and fuel processing are on-site or external; externalizing the load cannot improve the expanded system balance.

## Decision and outstanding evidence
Maintain a distinct route from compression-driven FRCs, but avoid treating the two as independent technologies without shared-risk analysis.

Outstanding: full relevant papers and supplements; a contradictory-results search; calibrated parameter distributions; cost/resource inventory; a robust region of coupled energy closure. Until these exist, no plant-specific success probability or comprehensive feasibility conclusion is justified.
