# Focused leading-candidate audit — C033: Indirect-drive inertial fusion with an efficient repetitive driver

This is a deeper **first audit**, not a completed extensive feasibility review. Current comparative score: 66.0/100. It is a single-analysis critical review, not independent peer review. See the full [concept dossier](../../TheoreticalConcepts/C033.md).

## Additional source question
References: R022 R001. Exact URLs and access limits are in [Bibliography](../../References/Bibliography.md).

Indirect-drive experiments furnish the clearest achieved target-gain milestone in this source set. That specific achievement is why omission of this baseline from the first portfolio needed correction.

## Challenge to the theoretical foundation
A hohlraum adds a conversion/coupling chain. Replacing a low-efficiency research laser with a hypothetical efficient driver cannot be assumed to preserve the target irradiation history and gain.

## Required mathematical investigation
Use separate efficiencies for wall-to-laser, laser-to-radiation and radiation-to-capsule, with a single consistent target-gain boundary; derive feasible combined ranges before assigning useful output.

The executed E001 model supplies only a necessary electrical threshold. It supplies no architecture-specific confinement result. A future coupled model must include input bounds, units, source provenance, numerical residuals, sensitivity and an independent algebraic balance. Matching one published optimum is inadequate calibration.

## Skeptical integration review
Target supply, chamber clearing, driver optical damage, low repetition and maintenance remain in the electric ledger even if every individual shot has target gain above unity.

Require a common comparison service: delivered electricity across at least a maintenance cycle. Report net on-state and mean net separately. State whether target supply, cryogenics and fuel processing are on-site or external; externalizing the load cannot improve the expanded system balance.

## Decision and outstanding evidence
Enter at 66/100 on initial screening, third among plant candidates. This is inclusion of missing positive evidence, not proof that it surpasses other plants.

Outstanding: full relevant papers and supplements; a contradictory-results search; calibrated parameter distributions; cost/resource inventory; a robust region of coupled energy closure. Until these exist, no plant-specific success probability or comprehensive feasibility conclusion is justified.
