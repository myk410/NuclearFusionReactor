# C001 — High-field D–T tokamak

Status: preliminary dossier, 2026-11-24. Type: **plant**. A candidate plant architecture; its subsystem dependencies still require integration.

## 1. Executive summary
Use externally produced toroidal and poloidal fields and alpha self-heating; seek compact burning plasma using high-field superconductors.

## 5. Mathematical justification and limits
At fixed beta and temperature, P_f/V scales approximately as B^4; test this against coil stress proportional to B² and the whole-plant Q_min. Units and common balance derivation are in E001. No numerical plasma prediction or concept-specific simulation has been performed. The relation establishes a testable necessary screen, not a sufficient existence proof.
