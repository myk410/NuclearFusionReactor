# C033 — Indirect-drive inertial fusion with an efficient repetitive driver

Status: preliminary dossier, 2026-11-24. Type: **plant**. A candidate plant architecture; its subsystem dependencies still require integration.

## 1. Executive summary
Convert external driver energy into a radiation field that compresses a fuel capsule; evaluate whether demonstrated target ignition can scale to efficient repeat operation.
